About me

Hi, I’m Jia. I’m a Research Scientist at Northeastern University, working on human–computer interaction for health — in particular, how it can help measure brain aging: cognition and mental health. Both are usually measured only in the clinic, in short visits. I work on what can be measured in everyday life instead, and on how people use the systems that do it.

I trained as an attending physician in neurology at Beijing Tiantan Hospital, Capital Medical University. My earlier research was on stroke, cerebrovascular disease, and cognition-related disorders.

Research interests

HCI for health. Designing and studying interactive systems for health assessment, and how patients, older adults, and clinicians actually use them.

Cognition and brain aging. Objective, repeatable markers of cognitive change drawn from everyday behavior, alongside conventional assessment.

Mental health monitoring. Low-burden, longitudinal assessment outside the clinic rather than at a single visit.
